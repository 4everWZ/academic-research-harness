# From Results to a Paper

## Build the argument from the completed work

Start with the author's question and supplied findings. Organize evidence around the central answer they support. If an early slogan no longer fits, propose a framing grounded in completed work without redefining the method or evaluation.

Settle a full paper's argument before polishing sections; local edits use the established argument. An outline is optional.

Apply [writing-style.md](writing-style.md) throughout drafting: frame contributions through the research object, model and findings, without disclaiming unmade claims.

When necessary values, comparisons or definitions are missing, ask the author and pause the dependent passage. Do not silently omit missing columns or present partial evidence as complete. Keep gap reports such as "data are missing, so no claim is made" outside the manuscript. Continue only independent writing; identify unfinished scope in the conversation.

Order evidence by its role: establishing the finding, explaining a design choice or locating a boundary or cost. Let mixed results shape the thesis rather than relegating exceptions to Limitations.

## Use each result for the question it answers

| Writing situation | Useful move |
|---|---|
| Main benchmark table | Select the comparisons that decide the research question. Distinguish consistent gains from gains concentrated in a condition; do not let an average obscure a reversal that changes the thesis. |
| Component ablation | Quantify the component's effect under the tested removal or replacement. Discuss a mechanism only to the extent established by the study; a scoped ablation finding needs no extra mechanism disclaimer. |
| Robustness or transfer | Organize by the shift or condition where behavior changes. Make adaptation inputs, such as labeled target examples, part of the interpretation when they determine what kind of robustness was evaluated. |
| Efficiency comparison | Connect the observed quality gain to its measured cost. Equal parameter count does not cancel higher latency; a measured latency difference should not be converted into an untested deployment verdict. |
| Sensitivity analysis | Explain where the conclusion holds, weakens, or reverses. Use this to locate an operating range rather than describing every curve point. |
| Null or uncertain result | State what remains unresolved and how that affects the paper's central answer. Do not turn lack of established improvement into equivalence or add generic future-work prose. |

## Carry the same argument across sections

State contributions as established research advances, not inventories of modules or experiments. A proposed framing does not authorize changes to research meaning.

Build Methods around scientific objects and operations. Explain component rationale and settings where needed for interpretation or reproduction, without reconstructing the source tree.

Use Discussion to explain what the combined findings change about the motivating problem. Distinguish observations from proposed explanations.

Derive abstract and conclusion from that argument, integrating material resource requirements, exceptions and uncertainty. Limitations explain specific assumptions and consequences without generic disclaimers. Apply structure and reporting rules for the supplied target outlet.
