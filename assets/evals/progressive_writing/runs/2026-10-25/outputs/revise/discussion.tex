\section{Discussion}
The estimated reduction in calibration error is 0.003 (95\% confidence interval: $[-0.002,0.008]$), so improvement was not established. On the low-light subset, the proposed method has higher error than the baseline (0.142 versus 0.131). The response-time measurements characterize operation with a fixed communication delay of 20 ms. We propose evaluating adaptive delay compensation.
