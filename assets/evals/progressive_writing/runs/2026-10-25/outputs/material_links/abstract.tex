\begin{abstract}
We study classifier calibration by fitting a scalar temperature using 500 labeled target images while holding classifier parameters fixed. Evaluation on fixed test samples from three target domains shows a reduction in mean expected calibration error from 0.128 to 0.091, with unchanged classification accuracy. Latency increases from 12 to 16 ms per image at batch size one on the same GPU. These descriptive findings show improved calibration with labeled target data alongside increased inference cost.
\end{abstract}
